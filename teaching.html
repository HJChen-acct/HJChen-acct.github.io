Teaching

My goal as an accounting instructor is to help students develop technical competence and the judgment to keep learning as the field changes.

Teaching Philosophy

At UCI, I am currently serving as the instructor of record for Introductory Managerial Accounting and have served as a teaching assistant for Intermediate Accounting II across multiple quarters. As the instructor, I design the course, prepare and deliver lectures, develop assignments and assessments, and take responsibility for students’ learning throughout the quarter. My teaching emphasizes both technical competence and the judgment needed to understand how accounting numbers are constructed and used in decision-making. I also incorporate generative AI into class activities that require students to evaluate the reasoning and assumptions behind an answer rather than simply accept it. My extensive TA experience has given me opportunities to lead discussion and review sessions, explain difficult accounting concepts, and work individually with students during office hours. Student evaluations consistently highlight my preparation, accessibility, and ability to explain difficult material from multiple perspectives, with my most recent overall rating reaching 3.87 out of 4.00.

I have come to see teaching and research as the same activity pointed at different audiences. What I want students to carry out of my courses is command of the fundamentals and the judgment to question any conclusion that sounds more certain than the evidence behind it.
