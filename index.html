About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Accounting at UC Irvine's Paul Merage School of Business, where I expect to complete my degree in 2027. I see an important role of accounting research as providing evidence on these consequences and identifying trade-offs that may not be fully anticipated during the rulemaking process.

Using archival research methods, my current work examines the consequences of principles-based narrative disclosure reform and of financial reporting simplification. I also work with coauthors on the state of replication research in accounting, which has shaped how I think about research credibility and transparency in my own projects.

Before the Ph.D. I earned a Master of Finance at UC Irvine and a B.A. in Finance from the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen.
