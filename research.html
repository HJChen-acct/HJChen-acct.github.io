Research Agenda

My research interests lie in financial accounting and reporting, with a particular focus on the unintended consequences of regulatory changes. Regulators revise reporting requirements to improve information usefulness or reduce compliance costs. However, because managers respond strategically to the incentives created by new rules, the realized effects of regulation may extend beyond, or diverge from, its stated objectives. I view an important role of accounting research as providing evidence on these consequences and identifying trade-offs that may not be fully anticipated during the rulemaking process. Using archival research methods, my current research examines two related areas: (1) the consequences of principles-based narrative disclosure reform and (2) the consequences of financial reporting simplification. This statement describes my active projects and my plans to build a sustained research agenda examining how financial reporting regulation produces consequences beyond those anticipated by standard setters.
